The Siddhagiri Museum, also known as the Siddhagiri Gramjivan Museum, is located near Kolhapur in Maharashtra, India. It is a unique open-air museum that showcases rural life, traditions, and culture in India. Established within the premises of the Siddhagiri Math at Kaneri, the museum is a brainchild of Shri Adrushya Kadsiddheshwar Swamiji, aimed at preserving the essence of India's village lifestyle.
The museum features life-sized clay and fiber sculptures depicting scenes from rural life, such as farming, crafts, education, and festivals. It also highlights self-sustained villages and ancient professions. Spread across 7 acres, it provides a walk-through experience of India's traditional way of living, making it both educational and culturally enriching. The museum is surrounded by lush greenery, adding to its serene and immersive atmosphere.

Museum HighLights

Village Life Depictions: Over 300 life-sized sculptures showcasing traditional rural activities like farming, weaving, pottery, and festivals.
Self-Sustained Village Model: Illustrates the concept of an eco-friendly village, promoting sustainability and traditional practices.
Historical Professions: Displays of over 80 ancient professions, such as blacksmiths, barbers, and cobblers, providing insights into traditional livelihoods.
Educational Focus: Aimed at teaching younger generations about India's cultural heritage and rural roots. Natural Surroundings: The museum is located in a serene environment, surrounded by lush greenery and the peaceful premises of Siddhagiri Math.
