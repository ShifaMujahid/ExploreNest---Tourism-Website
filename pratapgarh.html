Pratapgad is a mountain fort located in Satara district, in the Western Indian state of Maharashtra. The fort is situated 24 kilometres from the Mahabaleshwar hill station. The fort is now a popular tourist destination.
The fort's historical significance is due to the Battle of Pratapgad, which took place here on 10 November 1659, between Chatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and Bijapur Sultanate general Afzal Khan.

Historical Significance

Pratapgad Fort was constructed in 1656 under the orders of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, who assigned his prime minister, Moropant Trimbak Pingle, to build it for defending the banks of the Nira and Koyna rivers and the Par pass. The fort gained historical significance during the Battle of Pratapgad on 10 November 1659, when Shivaji Maharaj's forces defeated Afzal Khan, a general of the Adil Shahi dynasty, marking a pivotal moment in the establishment of the Maratha Empire.
Over the years, Pratapgad remained a site of political intrigue. In 1778, Sakharam Bapu Bokil, a minister of Pune, was imprisoned there by his rival Nana Phadnis, who later used the fort as a strategic base during power struggles. Eventually, during the Third Anglo-Maratha War in 1818, the fort was surrendered to the British East India Company, marking the end of its Maratha rule.

Architectural Features

The fort is divided into two main sections: the Upper Fort at the hilltop and the lower fort along the slopes, providing extensive protection and surveillance. The fort’s massive defensive walls are constructed with stone, featuring loopholes for firing at invaders, and its main entrance gate is heavily fortified, ensuring the safety of its inhabitants. The fort also houses the Bhavani Mata Temple, built by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj to honor the goddess Bhavani, believed to protect the Maratha empire.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Pratapgad Fort is during the winter season (October to March) when the weather is cool and pleasant for sightseeing. The monsoon season (June to September) offers lush greenery but heavy rains may make exploration difficult..
