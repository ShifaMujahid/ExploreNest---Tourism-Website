The Fort is a popular weekend destination for many residents of Pune, including trekking enthusiasts with access to the top of the fort from the base of the Sinhagad village. The trek involves a one-way walk of 2.7 km (1.6 miles) over which the walker gains about 600 m (1950 feet) in elevation.

Historical Significance

Sinhagad Fort, originally known as Kondana, was captured by Muhammad bin Tughluq in 1328. It came under Maratha control in 1647 when Shivaji Maharaj took it from the Adilshahi. After multiple shifts in control, the fort was recaptured by Shivaji in 1670 through the Battle of Sinhagad, where his commander Tanaji Malusare died in battle. The fort changed hands several times between the Marathas and Mughals before remaining under Maratha control until it was seized by the British in 1817.

Architectural Features

Sinhagad Fort features strong defensive walls, with gates like Pune Darwazaand Kalyan Darwaza, designed for protection. It has high bastions and watchtowers for strategic surveillance. The fort houses temples like the Kali Temple, water tanks, and natural caves for storage. A memorial to Tanaji Malusare honors his sacrifice in the Battle of Sinhagad. Its steep, rugged location provides natural defenses, making it a formidable military stronghold.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Sinhagad Fort is during the winter months (November to February) when the weather is cool and pleasant, making it ideal for trekking and exploration. The skies are clear, and the temperatures range from 12°C to 25°C, ensuring a comfortable visit.
Monsoon (June to September) brings lush greenery and a refreshing atmosphere, but the trails can be slippery and challenging for hiking.
Summer (March to May) can be hot, with temperatures reaching up to 35°C, making it less ideal for outdoor activities.

Location

Sinhagad Fort is located near the town of Sinhagad, approximately 35 kilometers southwest of Pune, Maharashtra, India. The fort is situated on the Sahyadri mountain range and can be reached via the Pune-Sinhagad Road.
