National Museum Of Indian Cinema

The National Museum of Indian Cinema (NMIC) is located in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India, and is dedicated to preserving and showcasing the history of Indian cinema. Opened in 2019, it is housed in the iconic Regal Cinema Building and an adjacent modern structure, which together offer a unique space for the museum's exhibits. The museum is managed by the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, Government of India.
The museum presents the evolution of Indian cinema through a wide range of interactive exhibits, audio-visual displays, film posters, props, costumes, and historical memorabilia from Indian films. The museum also honors key figures in the industry, showcasing the contributions of legendary actors, directors, and technicians. Visitors can explore sections on the history of Indian cinema, from its beginnings in the silent film era to contemporary Bollywood and regional cinema. The museum offers a comprehensive view of the art of filmmaking, its cultural impact, and its role in shaping Indian society.

Museum HighLights

Historical Cinema Artifacts: A vast collection of posters, film reels, costumes, props, and photographs from Indian cinema’s history.
Interactive Exhibits: Engaging displays showcasing the evolution of filmmaking techniques, sound and editing technologies, and cinematic trends.
Iconic Films and Personalities: Dedicated sections celebrating legendary actors, directors, and producers who shaped Indian cinema.
Film Memorabilia: Rare collections of awards, manuscripts, and personal items belonging to famous film personalities.
Documentaries and Short Films: Visitors can watch informative films and documentaries on the history of cinema in India.
Virtual Experiences: Interactive setups that allow visitors to explore behind-the-scenes footage, film production techniques, and more.

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Vastu Sangrahalaya

  • visiting information
  • Best Time to Visit

The Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Vastu Sangrahalaya (formerly known as the Prince of Wales Museum) is a premier museum located in South Mumbai, Maharashtra. Established in 1922, it is a cultural and historical landmark that showcases India’s rich heritage and history. The museum is set in a magnificent building designed in the Indo-Saracenic architectural style, featuring domes, turrets, and intricate stonework.

Museum HighLights

Architectural Splendor: The museum building, designed in the Indo-Saracenic style, is adorned with a central dome and intricate carvings.
Ancient Sculptures: A vast collection of stone sculptures from various Indian dynasties, including Buddhist, Hindu, and Jain artifacts.
Miniature Paintings: A remarkable collection of Rajput, Mughal, and Deccan miniature paintings.
Arms and Armor: A display of ancient weapons, swords, shields, and firearms.
Natural History Section: Showcases taxidermy, geological specimens, and information on India’s biodiversity.
