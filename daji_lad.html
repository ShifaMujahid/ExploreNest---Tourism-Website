The Bhau Daji Lad Museum, located in Byculla, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India, is one of the oldest museums in Mumbai, originally established in 1855 as the Victoria and Albert Museum. It was renamed in 1975 in honor of Bhau Daji Lad, a prominent philanthropist and the first native to hold the post of honorary curator of the museum. The museum is dedicated to preserving and showcasing the rich cultural heritage and history of Mumbai and Maharashtra.

Museum HighLights

Historical Artifacts: The museum houses a significant collection of Indian artifacts, including sculptures, ceramics, and textiles, reflecting the rich cultural heritage of Mumbai and Maharashtra.
Historical Models: It features intricate models of Mumbai from the 19th century, showcasing the evolution of the city’s landscape and architecture.
Traditional Arts: The museum exhibits traditional Indian crafts, including woodwork, metalwork, and embroideries, offering a glimpse into India's artisanal history.
Exquisite Exhibits: It contains a stunning collection of palaeontological fossils, ancient manuscripts, and miniature paintings.
Restoration of Local Culture: Dedicated to the preservation of Mumbai’s cultural heritage, it offers educational programs and workshops.
