The highest fort in the Pune district. Torna Fort is the first fort captured by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj in 1646,at the age of 16. Known for its strategic location, the fort offers scenic views and is a popular trekking destination.The hill has an elevation of 1,403 metres (4,603 ft) above sea level, making it the highest hill-fort in the district.

Historical Significance

Torna Fort is believed to have been constructed in the 13th century by the Shiva Panth. It was later captured by Malik Ahmed, a Bahamani ruler, during the reign of Alauddin Khilji in the late 1470s. The fort has a Menghai Devi temple near its entrance. In 1646, Shivaji Maharaj captured the fort at the age of sixteen, renaming it Prachandagad and adding several monuments and towers, making it one of the first forts of the Maratha Empire.
In the 18th century, the fort briefly fell under Mughal control after the assassination of Sambhaji Maharaj. Aurangzeb, the Mughal emperor, renamed it Futulgaib, acknowledging the fort's strong defenses. However, the fort was later restored to the Maratha Confederacy through the Treaty of Purandar.

Architectural Features

Torna Fort has several key architectural features reflecting its strategic importance. The fort is surrounded by strong stone walls, providing robust defense. Its Rupee Darwaza and Toranjai Darwaza gates are designed for controlled access and security. The fort also includes watchtowers that offer expansive views of the surrounding area, aiding in surveillance and defense. At the entrance is the Menghai Devi temple, an important religious site. Additionally, water cisterns are present within the fort, designed to store water during sieges. Inside the fort, visitors can find ruins of towers and structures constructed by Shivaji Maharaj, making it a historically significant site for the Maratha Empire.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Sinhagad Fort is during the winter months (November to February) when the weather is cool and pleasant, making it ideal for trekking and exploration. The skies are clear, and the temperatures range from 12°C to 25°C, ensuring a comfortable visit.
Monsoon (June to September) brings lush greenery and a refreshing atmosphere, but the trails can be slippery and challenging for hiking.
Summer (March to May) can be hot, with temperatures reaching up to 35°C, making it less ideal for outdoor activities.

Location

The fort is about 50 km via Pabe ghat southwest of Pune in the Western Ghats of the Sahyadri mountain ranges at the base village Velhe. One can go from Pune via Satara road and take right at Nasarapur village. This distance is near about 65 km. It is the highest fort in the Pune district.
